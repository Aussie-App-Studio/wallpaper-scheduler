Privacy Policy

Wallpaper Scheduler: Time of Day (published by Aussie App Studio)

Effective date: November 17, 2026

1. Data Collection

Wallpaper Scheduler: Time of Day does not collect, store externally, sell, or transmit any personal information, usage analytics, crash telemetry, or device identifiers.

2. No Internet Access

The application declares no internet capability in its Windows app manifest (Package.appxmanifest). All wallpaper scheduling, folder ordering, and sunrise/sunset calculations run 100% locally on your device.

3. Local Storage

Your schedule configuration (selected file/folder paths, times, optional city/coordinates, and app preferences) is saved exclusively on your PC in %LocalAppData%\WallpaperScheduler\config.json.

4. External Links

The Settings page includes an optional link to a YouTube video. Clicking that link opens your default web browser outside the application.

5. Contact

Questions or feedback can be submitted via the GitHub repository.
